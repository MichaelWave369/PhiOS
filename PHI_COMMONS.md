# Phi Commons

PhiOS is part of the **Phi Commons**: a collection of free, inspectable, modifiable tools intended to increase human agency without requiring dependency on a single vendor, service, model provider, or account.

Unless a file or directory says otherwise, original Phi Commons software and associated documentation in this repository are released under the **MIT License** in `LICENSE`.

That means people may use, copy, modify, merge, publish, distribute, sublicense, and sell copies of the project-owned software, subject to the MIT notice requirements.

## What MIT does not relicense

The Phi Commons license applies only to material that the project has the right to license.

It does **not** replace or override:

- third-party dependency licenses;
- model-weight or dataset licenses;
- fonts, images, audio, or other assets with separate terms;
- copied or vendored upstream code carrying its own notice;
- trademarks, logos, or identity marks.

See `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` and `MODEL_LICENSES.md`.

## Commons boundary

The Phi Commons and Field Supply are intentionally separate.

Commercial Field Supply products are not automatically covered by this repository's MIT grant merely because they interoperate with PhiOS. Likewise, an open PhiOS capability does not gain authority over a commercial product simply because both are installed on the same machine.

## Sovereignty principle

Open availability is not execution authority.

PhiOS may be freely studied and modified while still enforcing:

- capability is not authority;
- privilege expands only by explicit grant;
- receipts precede promotion;
- failures remain failures;
- local user control remains the governing boundary.
